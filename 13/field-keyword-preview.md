# `field` keyword (preview in C# 13)

In C# 13 (Visual Studio 17.12+) `field` was a **preview** feature and needs `<LangVersion>preview</LangVersion>`.
It became stable in C# 14. Full notes: [../14/field-keyword.md](../14/field-keyword.md).

On a `net9.0` project without preview: do not use `field`; write a manual backing field.
